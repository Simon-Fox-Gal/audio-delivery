About the work

A cantata in five episodes

De Profundis was composed in 1936–37 and dedicated “To the memory of this time, its misery and its victims”. Gál assembled its German Baroque texts himself, drawing together spiritual and secular poetry on transience, death, human suffering, consolation and hope.

The five self-contained movements pass through reflection, earthly life, night, the dance of death and a final appeal for peace. The result has often been described as a kind of spiritual symphony: deeply existential, but without dogmatic religious intent.

First performance: Wiesbaden, March 1948, conducted by Otto Schmidtgen. Despite its scale and historical importance, the work has never received a commercial recording.
